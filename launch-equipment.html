The ISISPACE deployers are the world-leading and most flown CubeSat deployers, developed to accommodate any type of CubeSat or nanosatellite, from 1U up to 16U, onboard a large variety of launch vehicles. Custom formats and volumes can be quickly realized. Fully qualified with flight heritage on various different launch vehicles: PSLV, Soyuz, Dnepr, Antares, Vega, LM-2D, LM-11, Atlas V, and Falcon 9.
